# Code of conduct

GeoModel3D follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). In short: be respectful and constructive, welcome people of every background and level of experience, and keep discussions about the work.

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainer, Kursat Kilic, at [kilic_kursat@hotmail.com](mailto:kilic_kursat@hotmail.com). Reports are treated in confidence.

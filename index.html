GeoModel3D

3D geological models from borehole logs and CPTs, in the browser.

Import borehole logs (CSV, AGS4, BRO, DOV, KuniJiban XML) and cone penetration tests (GEF, BRO, DOV, AGS4), or type them in. GeoModel3D builds the layers between the boreholes and cuts sections through them at any angle or along a tunnel or road alignment. It shows groundwater and effective stresses, and exports reports, sections and 3D models. It is free and open source, and your data are not uploaded anywhere.

# Independent engineering review of Tochigi project 119010496

Record the intended engineering use and the acceptable errors in position, ground level, measured layer boundaries and predicted contact elevation before opening any model results. Use the six PDFs numbered `11901049601.pdf` through `11901049606.pdf` from Tochigi Prefecture's first archive. Verify their hashes before reviewing. The publisher is 栃木県 県土整備部 (Tochigi Prefecture, Department of Land Development). The reports are in Japanese and licensed under CC BY 2.1 Japan. [Original archive](https://data.bodik.jp/dataset/a6ee71a9-c0d4-4386-803d-e588fb0ed7ca/resource/4cc62581-9adf-4aaa-a970-79dd240494f4/download/72402_2014_boring-log1_04010.zip) · [Dataset and licence declaration](https://data.bodik.jp/dataset/090000_boring-log) · [Licence](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.1/jp/) · [PDF checksums](SHA256SUMS.txt)

1. For an independent prediction, set aside `11901049603.pdf` unopened. Use the other five PDFs in a fresh project. Record the coordinate system, material grouping, repeated beds and any manual decisions. Freeze the model and its predictions for ground and the top of the Gravel unit at the held-out collar before reading the held-out geology. A colleague can supply the printed collar coordinates alone, or you can read only that header.
2. Read all six source logs and complete the blank worksheet. Record each log's identifier, coordinates and angular precision, horizontal and vertical references, collar elevation, drilled length and depth to every change of material. Inspect each continuation page. Add rows for individual boundaries when field values come from different pages. Include a column or region reference for ambiguous values. [Blank worksheet](worksheet.csv)
3. For the held-out log, record the depth and elevation at the top of the first bed explicitly labelled 砂礫 (GS). Assess whether the evidence supports correlating that bed across the other five logs. Record signed errors as predicted elevation minus observed elevation. Identify interpretations that depend on uncertain datum, sparse coverage or repeated gravel beds.
4. Record your name, professional role, review date, app version and any limitations on use. Compare the independently recorded observations with the model results only after the worksheet and acceptance criteria are complete. A signed assessment can cover the specific use and site you reviewed.

## Assigning interpreted beds in the five training holes

Complete your independent transcription first. The assignment worksheet contains the printed observations for 38 intervals in the five training holes, including Japanese soil names, classification codes and source pages. Each row covers one measured interval. A repeated name or code can occur at several depths. The cross-hole interpretation columns are empty. [Training bed assignment worksheet](training-bed-assignments.csv)

1. Import the five training PDFs and all their pages into an empty project. Verify the measured intervals against the original sheets, then save the five-hole project. Use a separate copy for your correlation work. Keep the sixth PDF out of the project until the interpretation and its predictions are frozen.
2. For an interval you can correlate, enter its interpreted bed ID in `reviewed_bed`, its position in the complete top-to-bottom unit list in `bed_rank` (1 is shallowest), and the geological basis in `review_reason`. Use the same bed ID and rank where you judge multiple intervals to represent the same bed. Give separated beds distinct IDs. Adjacent intervals can belong to the same bed while retaining their own source boundaries. A blank decision leaves its interval available for later review.
3. Keep the identifying columns intact. `material_id` records the source material group and `expected_bed` identifies its current interpretation in this worksheet's starting project. `source_sha256` refers to the unmodified original PDF, so the worksheet can be used after a fresh import of the same five files. The source pages and Japanese labels help you check the evidence. `expert_confidence` is an optional worksheet note. Keep the completed CSV with your review record.
4. Import the completed CSV against that same five-hole project. Inspect the measured intervals, interpreted beds and collar discrepancies before scoring the withheld hole. The frozen source checks establish which intervals were read. Distinguishing their continuity across holes still requires your engineering interpretation.

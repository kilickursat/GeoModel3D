# GeoModel3D 0.32.0 plan: reading scanned logs

This plan comes from measuring the 0.31.0 reader on the public benchmark (`bench/`), with every page read. It lists the causes of the corrections that remain, the fixes for them in order, and what was tested before writing it down. The causes were diagnosed on the dev and validation reports only. Nothing in the app is changed yet.

## Causes found

**1. USGS: the publisher's hidden OCR text has no column headings.** All 145 pages are scans with an invisible text layer (PDF text render mode 3) drawn over the scanned image. The reader takes that layer as the page's text, because most of it reads as words. The blow counts are in it and mostly right (log 79040: 4, 3, 17, 33, 17, 22, 8, 26, 15, 13, as in the answer key). But the column headings are printed sideways and are missing from the layer, so no column is known to hold blow counts, and none of the 126 tests is read. The app's own OCR reads the headings: "Depth (feet)", "Water", "Blow … (blows/ft)", "Water Content". The reader's heading pattern does not accept "Blow" alone or "(blows/ft)" in brackets.

**2. Hirosaki: the OCR finds the small text but misreads it.**
- These scans were published at 150 dpi (A4 at 1240 × 1753 pixels, A3 at 2480 × 1753), the resolution the app reads at.
- On four dev logs, 67 of 96 SPT depth labels appear in the OCR text. At the place of a lost label there is nearly always a misread one: "16.11" for 14.15, "10.15" for 13.15, "1u" or "1.技" for 1.15.
- Soil names and remarks show the same misreads: 火山灰質 → 火山灰賀, 火山灰 → 火山厌, 含水 → 合水, 混入 → 温入, 黒ボク → 馬ポク.
- Names of neighbouring layers are joined into one string where one OCR box spans several cells of the name column ("じ質粘礫混じり火山灰質粘土粘土混じり砂礫").
- **Tested and rejected:** enlarging the page 2× before OCR. It found fewer labels (57 of 96) and took longer (117–163 s a page instead of 81–108 s). The limit is the recognition model at this resolution, not the detection of text or the rendering.

**3. Water on Japanese scans: a test depth is taken for the water level.** 13 of the 14 Hirosaki boreholes get a wrong water level, 11 of them exactly the first test depth (1.15 or 1.45). On born-digital logs, 28 of 29 recorded levels are right and the other is missed; none is wrong.

**4. Material: 126 layers wrong on dev and validation.**
- **71: the name is misread, joined, or on the wrong layer.** These are mostly OCR on the Hirosaki scans. On some Tochigi 120 logs (validation), each layer gets the next layer's name.
- **31: classification policy, not reading.** The name is read right, but the app and the answer keys group it differently: 火山灰質粘土 is volcanic ash soil in the app and clay in the keys; 有機質粘土 is organic in the app and clay in the keys; 固結粘土 is "rock and cemented soil" in the app and clay in the keys.
- **19: no layer read there** (missed pages and top layers).
- **5: a word in the remarks decides the class.** "固結状の凝灰質粘土にて充填" (the gravel's matrix is cemented clay) makes a gravel layer rock. In `unitFor` (`src/docread/draft.ts`), when the first word is not a soil name, the Japanese rules are applied to the whole description, and the rules for 有機質, 火山灰 and 固結 match anywhere in it.

**5. RQD written in the remarks is not read.** For example, "RQDは3-4m:10、4-5m:39、5-6m:40" (Tochigi 117: six values).

## Fixes, in order

Steps 1–4 first. They are tested or are clear bugs.

**1. Read scans with a hidden OCR layer by the app's own OCR, and accept the "Blow" headings.**
- **Where:**
  - in `src/docread/pdftext.ts` (judging a page's text) and `src/docread/index.ts` (routing), a page whose text is all drawn invisible over an image is read by OCR, with the reason "a scan whose text is another program's OCR";
  - in `testsByColumn` (`src/docread/reconstruct.ts`), the heading pattern;
  - the same rule in `judge()` of `bench/runner/read.ts`.
- **Tested** on the 10 USGS pages in the answer key, with a scratch copy of the runner and the pattern below. On the 6 keyed boreholes it assembled (115 tests), 92 are right, 1 count is wrong, 22 are missed, and 14 tests are read that the key does not list. Before, 0 were read.
- **Cost:** every page of such a report is read by OCR. That is 145 pages for this report, at about 1.5 minutes a page on a processor without a graphics card, and much faster with WebGPU. A later saving: use the hidden text to pick the log pages, and OCR only those.
- **Check:** USGS SPT at least as above, after a complete run; no change on the other reports, nor on the PDFs used to check earlier releases.

**2. Water: only a level written with its date, never a test depth.**
- **Where:** `waterColumn` (`src/docread/reconstruct.ts`). Take a value from the 孔内水位 column only where its measuring date (月日, "9/5") is beside it, or at least where it is none of the test depths read on the page. Otherwise leave the level empty.
- **Check:** no wrong water level on the Hirosaki scans; the born-digital results unchanged (28 of 29).

**3. Classify a Japanese layer by its soil name only.**
- **Where:** `unitFor` (`src/docread/draft.ts`) and the rule order in `japaneseLithologyRules` (`src/rules.ts`). Apply the rules to the soil name (the first word, without its bracket code), where the last noun decides. Where the first word is not a soil name, never class the layer from any word in its remarks: leave the unit for the review.
- **Check:** the five layers made rock by their remarks; no born-digital layer worse.

**4. Names by the drawn cells, and OCR text split at the drawn lines.**
- **Where:** `groupNames` and the description step of `rebuild` (`src/docread/reconstruct.ts`): a name belongs to the cell between the lines drawn across the name column, not to the nearest boundary. For OCR, cut a detected text box where a drawn line crosses it, before recognition (`src/docread/ocr.worker.ts`, `src/docread/ocrclient.ts`, with the page's lines passed in), or recognise each cell of the name column on its own.
- **Develop** on dev pages (Hirosaki Ringo has the joined names). Confirm on validation (Tochigi 120 has the shifted names) without tuning on it.
- **Check:** no joined names on the dev scans; the shifted names right on Tochigi 120.

Then, measured on dev and confirmed on validation:

**5. Correct a misread test depth from the depth scale.** On JACIC forms the test depths are x.15 and x.45, and the depth scale gives each row's depth from its place on the page. Where the label is misread ("16.11"), put the depth from the scale and flag it ("read as 16.11; depth from the scale"). Never add a test that was not read.

**6. Correct OCR misreads against a word list** of the terms of Japanese logs (含水, 混入, 火山灰, 黒ボク, 粘性, 風化…), flagged as corrected.

**7. Read RQD from the remarks** ("RQD", then depth ranges and values).

## Decisions for the owner

- **Classification policy (31 of the 126 material errors).** Choose once, then make the app and the answer keys follow it.
  - Suggested: the major groups of JGS 0051, under which 有機質粘土 is an organic soil and 火山灰質粘土 a volcanic cohesive soil. That is the app's grouping today, so this mostly changes the keys.
  - For 固結粘土, keep the app's "rock and cemented soil", or class it with clay as the keys do.
- **Test split.** All four keyed test reports were debugged while making 0.31.0, so the test split is no longer held out. Suggested: move them to dev or validation, and make the three test reports not yet keyed (jp-neyagawa-clean-center, us-usgs-ofr94-706, us-usgs-ofr2011-1281) and any new ones the held-out set. Those three have never been run through the debugging tools.
- **OCR time.** Whether an hour or more of OCR on the processor is acceptable for a large report of scans with hidden OCR text, or whether such reports should be read by OCR only on request.

## Models: not in this release

- **Layout models** (docling-layout-heron, PP-DocLayout, YOLO-style segmentation) are not needed. None of the remaining errors comes from finding the regions of a page, and the drawn lines of a form already give its cells.
- **Higher resolution does not help** (tested, above).
- **One experiment is worth doing after fixes 5 and 6:** a larger PP-OCR recognition model for low-resolution Japanese scans. Swap only the recognition model in the worker, and measure test depths, blow counts, soil names and seconds per page on the dev scans. Adopt it only if it clearly beats the code corrections: it means a bigger download and slower pages.
- **Vision-language models** (PaddleOCR-VL, LightOnOCR) not now. They are too large and slow for the browser, and they can produce plausible values that are not on the page. If tried later, it should be as an optional reader for layouts not seen before, judged on the benchmark's unsafe-build count.

## Benchmark fixes

- **One-foot drives.** `bench/runner/read.ts` scales a test driven 12 in (30.48 cm) to 30 cm, so a count of 33 is scored as 32. A one-foot drive is the standard test, and the app already counts any drive of 300 mm or more as complete (`sptN`, `src/schema.ts`). Change `Math.abs(pen-30)<0.5` to `pen>=29.5` in the runner. No reported score changes, because no feet log had its tests read before.
- **Plan again whenever the reader changes.** Run `plan` and `ocr.mjs` again before `read` (the README now says so), and check that `read` reports no pages "not yet read by OCR".
- **Still open:** verify the answer keys by hand, build a new held-out test split, and add more report formats, which is the main lever for reading reports not seen before.

## Acceptance

## Tested code from the experiments

The rule for scans with hidden OCR text, as tried in a scratch copy of `judge()` (it tracks the text render mode through save and restore):

```ts
const ops=await page.getOperatorList(),O=pdfjs.OPS,stack:number[]=[];
let mode=0,shown=0,hidden=0,images=0;
for(let i=0;i<ops.fnArray.length;i++){
  const f=ops.fnArray[i];
  if(f===O.save)stack.push(mode);
  else if(f===O.restore)mode=stack.pop()??mode;
  else if(f===O.setTextRenderingMode)mode=ops.argsArray[i][0];
  else if(f===O.showText||f===O.showSpacedText){if(mode===3)hidden++;else shown++}
  else if(f===O.paintImageXObject||f===O.paintInlineImageXObject||f===O.paintImageMaskXObject)images++;
}
const hiddenOcr=hidden>0&&shown===0&&images>0;   // read this page by OCR
```

The heading pattern tried in `testsByColumn`:

```ts
/^\(?(?:blow\s*count|blows?(?:\s*\/\s*(?:ft|foot|0\.3\s*m))?|spt(?:\s*n)?|n[- ]?value)\)?(?:\s|$)/i
```

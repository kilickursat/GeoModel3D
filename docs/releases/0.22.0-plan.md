# GeoModel3D 0.22.0 implementation plan

This milestone adds two separate, auditable calculations to the saved-interpretation workflow.

## Contact interval calibration

- Partition physical observation groups into training, calibration and audit sets using a recorded seed. Equivalent IDs, moved collars and co-located borehole/CPT records stay together across every interpretation.
- Fit the selected interpretations using training observations only. Keep calibration and audit observations out of all fitted cases.
- Calibrate the training envelope with split-conformal scores `max(lower − observed, observed − upper, 0)` and order statistic `ceil((n + 1) × target coverage)`. An out-of-range rank or unbounded score must never become an invented finite interval.
- Draw the calibrated training-only intervals separately from full-data model disagreement. Export group assignments, removals, calibration scores, interval settings and untouched audit performance.
- Explain marginal coverage under exchangeability and fixed assumptions. Spatial dependence, prior interpretation choices and tuning after inspecting audit results limit that interpretation.

## Investigation cost and information

- Treat the selected cases as a finite hypothesis set with explicit prior weights. A hypothetical observation reports a contact-elevation bin with a stated resolution and origin.
- Calculate exact expected conditional entropy reduction after earlier proposed observations. Redundant observations add zero information; total information cannot exceed prior case entropy.
- Rank affordable candidates greedily by conditional bits per estimated cost. Enforce both the monetary budget and maximum location count, physical spacing and recorded-collar exclusions.
- Estimate costs from a stated fixed charge and drilling rate. Estimated investigation depths must reach the chosen contact in every selected case, plus the chosen buffer and any deeper design target.
- Export costs, remaining budget, gains, bins and all assumptions. This is scenario discrimination under a noiseless observation model, not a calibrated probability of geological correctness or a claim of globally optimal drilling design.

## Delivery and acceptance

- Integrate both calculations into cancelable workers, saved-revision controls, CSV/JSON exports and the standalone HTML.
- Preserve measured observations, saved revisions, inactive scenario drafts and existing workflows.
- Verify analytic quantiles and entropy examples, leakage prevention, independent audit behavior, missing-data handling, budget and depth constraints, and input immutability.
- Run the complete unit suite, TypeScript checks, production build and desktop/mobile/offline Playwright checks. Inspect screenshots of the new flows.
- Update package, lockfile, citation, guide, methods and release notes to 0.22.0. Commit with the requested author, push a reviewable PR and verify CI before merge/release.

References: Angelopoulos and Bates, [A Gentle Introduction to Conformal Prediction](https://arxiv.org/abs/2107.07511); Lindley (1956), [On a Measure of the Information Provided by an Experiment](https://doi.org/10.1214/aoms/1177728069).

## Acceptance results

Implementation and local validation are complete: 569 unit tests, all 44 Playwright cases, TypeScript checks and the production/standalone build pass. All five affected browser cases pass again after the final compact-CSV change. Independent numerical checks cover every one of the seven reference/demo sites without changing source inputs.

The four-case synthetic planning example buys two complementary observations for 340 cost units, obtains exactly two conditional bits and stops before buying redundant observations. The same plan buys no locations with a budget of 169. Analytic tests verify nonzero conformal expansion, audit independence, unbounded scores, pinched-out contacts and depth/cost constraints across different scenario ground levels.

A real Antwerp calibration export is 197 KB rather than 23.31 MB after storing global CSV metadata once. The numerical report and JSON archive are unchanged. Screenshots use synthetic study data in Chromium desktop and Pixel 7 mobile emulation:
